About Us

Securing Your Property With Integrity

Your trusted geodetic surveying partner in Zambales.

Gramm Geodetic Surveying Development Inc. is a professional surveying firm based in San Marcelino, Zambales. We specialize in providing accurate, reliable, and timely geodetic surveying services to homeowners, developers, and businesses throughout the region.

Our commitment to excellence and integrity ensures that every property transaction and survey is handled with the utmost care and precision. From relocation surveys to land titling, we are your one-stop solution for all your surveying needs.
