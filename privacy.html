1. Introduction

GRAMM Geodetic Surveying Development Inc. ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the guidelines of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or contact us for surveying services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide through our contact form or WhatsApp inquiries:

  • Contact Details: Name, email address, phone number
  • Property Information: Location, type of survey service requested
  • Inquiry Details: Any additional information you share in your message

We do not collect sensitive personal information (e.g., government IDs, financial records) through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide service quotations
  • To communicate with you regarding survey scheduling and deliverables
  • To coordinate with local government units if required for permit/title processing
  • To improve our website functionality and customer support

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Your data may only be shared:

  • With our licensed surveying team to fulfill your request
  • When required by Philippine law or government agencies (e.g., Registry of Deeds, LGU)
  • With your explicit written consent

We use secure third-party tools (WhatsApp, Gmail, Formspree) solely to facilitate communication and form submissions.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no online transmission is 100% secure, and we encourage you to exercise caution when sharing information digitally.

6. Your Rights Under Philippine Law

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or withdraw consent for data processing
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) if your rights are violated
